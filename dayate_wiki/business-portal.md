# Business portal

Shows dashboard data for the signed-in business account.

## Frontend

`App.jsx` (`/business/dashboard`) → `pages/BusinessDashboard.jsx` → `services/businessDashboardService.js`

## Backend

`server.js` (`/api/business`) → `modules/business/business.routes.js` → `business.controller.js` → `business.service.js`

Authentication belongs to [accounts](accounts.md).
