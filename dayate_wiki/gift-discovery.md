# Gift discovery

Finds nearby or searched gift shops and loads their photos.

## Frontend

`App.jsx` (`/gifts`) → `pages/Gifts.jsx` → `hooks/useNearbyGiftShops.js` → `services/giftService.js`

## Backend

`server.js` (`/api/gifts`) → `modules/gifts/gift.routes.js` → `gift.controller.js` → `gift.service.js`
