# Admin portal

Shows platform metrics and reviews business approvals.

## Frontend

`App.jsx` (`/admin/dashboard`) → `pages/AdminDashboard.jsx` → `services/adminDashboardService.js`

## Backend

`server.js` (`/api/admin`) → `modules/admin/admin.routes.js` → `admin.controller.js` → `admin.service.js`

Authentication belongs to [accounts](accounts.md).
